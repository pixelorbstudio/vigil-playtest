# Sound effects in this folder

- **crt-on.wav** (1.5 s): the monitor and the room powering on, as the shift starts (the start's pull-back, and the power knob). Layered from two recordings, both Creative Commons 0 (public domain, no attribution required):
  - "CRT TV Switches On" by Fission9 (https://freesound.org/people/Fission9/sounds/693860/): the static fizz of the switch-on (0.215 to 0.575 s, highs only over 2.2 kHz, 30 ms fade in, 7 dB down) and the tube's line whine (1.20 to 2.40 s, highs only over 11 kHz, fading in over 0.35 s from 0.22 s, 2 dB down).
  - "CRT computer monitor startup" by corkob (https://freesound.org/people/corkob/sounds/415594/): the degauss, its 50 Hz buzz (1.90 to 2.95 s, from 30 ms, swelling in over 140 ms instead of its own instant hit, decaying over 0.75 s, 10 dB down).
  - Measured in the page as the straps were (audit-sound.js's meter): -40.2 dBA, unweighted -35, peak 0.065; a keystroke -36.5 dBA, unweighted -36.4, peak 0.18. Built 2026-10-12; the first version (the switch-on's snap alone) sounded like a bang to Arnold: its instant attack, which this one has none of.
