# Sound effects in this folder

- **crt-on.wav** (2.7 s): the tube coming on as the shift starts (the start's pull-back, and the power knob): a switch click, a burst of static that fades over about a second, a small second click. It is power_on.mp3 from George Merlocco's retro-crt-startpage (https://github.com/scar45/retro-crt-startpage, src/power_on.mp3), the repository is marked MIT on GitHub; its licence file (LICENSE-retro-crt-startpage.txt beside this one) is the MIT notice of HTML5 Boilerplate, the template it was built from, and the repository doesn't say where the sound itself came from. Converted to 44.1 kHz mono WAV and 2.5 dB down, nothing else changed: -37.9 dBA in the page, unweighted -35.5, peak 0.12; a keystroke -36.9 dBA, unweighted -36.9, peak 0.18 (audit-sound.js's meter). Chosen 2026-10-12 by Arnold's word ("just nab a good one"), after a CRT's switch-on snap alone (a bang to him) and a layered power-up (he didn't like it).
